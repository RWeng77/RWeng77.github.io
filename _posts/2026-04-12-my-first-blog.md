## Why start writing blog?

To be honest, I'm a very lazy person and don't like **taking notes**.

Even when I do, they are just scattered records without any systematic organization.

That was until last year. I won't go into the details of why, I was told that if you want people to see the efforts you've made, you need to record them. Whether it's a huge success or just a small thing.

So, here it is. Even though it's a year late, it's better than not working on it, right? I'll keep updating this blog and post some research I did previously.
